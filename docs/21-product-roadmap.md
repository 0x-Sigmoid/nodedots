# 21 · Product Roadmap

Version: 0.1 · Status: outcome-based proposal · Owner: Product

## Roadmap policy

Sequence capabilities by demonstrated value, graph reliability, evaluation readiness, and operational cost. Phases are not delivery commitments. Shared evidence and tenant controls remain prerequisites throughout. Expanding domains must not dilute the initial PR intelligence proposition.

## Phase 1 · Validate PR intelligence

Build organization/install flow, selected repositories, supported baseline graph, PR snapshots, deterministic rules, bounded AI candidates, evidence validator, advisory checks, reports, feedback, deletion, and operational visibility.

Exit: mandatory [PRD](02-product-requirements-document.md) behavior passes; [beta](20-launch-and-beta-plan.md) supplies sufficient usefulness/precision evidence. If precision is weak, improve a few rule families instead of broadening product scope.

## Phase 2 · Enter the shipping workflow

Add a read-only CLI, explicit CI integration, repository policy configuration, stronger test-gap analysis, supported migration/backfill checks, and API-contract checks. Begin paid plans only with approved commercial terms and reliable metering.

Dependencies: stable shared analysis API, local privacy model, CI credential design, signed/reproducible report contracts, and rule-specific evaluations. Exit: repeat use in real pre-merge workflows without unacceptable false-positive burden. Required merge gates remain separately opt-in and need stronger failure semantics.

## Phase 3 · System intelligence

Add useful architecture exploration, relationship history, linked architectural decisions, onboarding explanations, and “Why does this exist?” with historical evidence. Explore cross-repository mapping with explicit installation/organization access boundaries.

Dependencies: stable logical entity identity, versioned snapshots, bounded history storage, and verified source-of-rationale links. Exit: teams can answer real architecture questions with traceable paths and history. Do not present inferred rationale as a recorded historical decision.

## Phase 4 · AI agent verification

Accept explicit developer intent plus agent changes and repository context. Provide read-only verification APIs for coding environments, intent coverage reports, and policy-aware before-shipping checklists.

Dependencies: requirement extraction, intent-to-implementation benchmarks, scoped token authentication, agent abuse controls, and clear separation between NodeDots verification and agent execution. Exit: measurable intent-level gaps beyond conventional diff review.

## Phase 5 · Selected non-code modes

Validate one of Apply, Contracts, or Research through a focused domain pilot. Reuse ingestion/provenance/relationships/states, but introduce domain-specific entity models, consent, evaluation, UX, and risk review.

Choose the mode using customer demand, access to trustworthy evidence, useful deterministic checks, and willingness to pay. Launching all three together is outside this roadmap's intent.

## Phase 6 · Process and decision intelligence

Explore Business, Verify, and Decisions after domain engine maturity. Nigerian small-business processes may be an initial Business focus, with official source freshness and process versioning as first-class requirements. Verification must distinguish support, conflict, missing context, and uncertainty; it cannot collapse every claim into true/false.

## Prioritization and review

Review quarterly and after major beta evidence. Score proposals on meaningful gaps resolved, precision risk, evidence availability, workflow fit, engineering effort, and operating cost. Record dependency, owner, hypothesis, success signal, and stop condition. Keep deferred features visibly deferred in documentation and marketing.
